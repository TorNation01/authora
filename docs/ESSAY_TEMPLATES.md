# Essay Templates

## Overview

11 essay templates for academic and course writing. Each includes structure, section scaffolds, guidance text, AI prompt hooks, citation integration, and export recommendations.

## Planning Sections (All Essay Types)

- **Topic & Research Question** – Narrow focus. One clear question or claim.
- **Thesis Statement** – One sentence. Debatable, specific, supportable.
- **Main Points / Outline** – 2–5 main points. One paragraph per point.
- **Counterarguments** – (if applicable) Anticipate objections.
- **Sources & References** – Key sources. Citation style.
- **Revision Notes** – Clarity, coherence, argument strength.

## AI Prompt Hooks

| Hook | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `thesis` | Refine thesis to be more specific and arguable |
| `outline` | Develop main points that support thesis |
| `transition` | Write transitions between paragraphs |
| `conclusion` | Strengthen conclusion without new ideas |

## Milestones

1. Thesis and outline complete  
2. First draft complete  
3. Citations added  
4. Revision complete  
5. Final polish  

## Export Recommendations

- **DOCX** – Primary. Citation-ready.
- **PDF** – Submission format.

## Academic Tone

- Formal third person unless instructed otherwise
- Objective presentation of evidence
- Precise vocabulary; define technical terms on first use
