# Research Writing Templates

## Overview

11 research and academic writing templates for papers, literature reviews, theses, journal articles, and methodology sections. Each includes IMRaD-compatible structure where applicable, citation integration, and export readiness.

## Template List

| Slug | Name | Structure |
|------|------|-----------|
| `academic-research-paper` | Research Paper | Abstract, Intro, Lit Review, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, References |
| `academic-research-lit-review` | Literature Review | Intro, Theme 1–3, Synthesis & Gap, Conclusion, References |
| `academic-research-annotated-bib` | Annotated Bibliography | Source 1–5+ with citation + annotation |
| `academic-research-thesis` | Thesis/Dissertation Planning | Abstract, Ch 1–6, References, Appendices |
| `academic-research-capstone` | Capstone Project Report | Exec Summary, Intro, Background, Methodology, Implementation, Evaluation, Conclusion, References |
| `academic-research-journal` | Journal Article | Abstract, Intro, Lit/Theory, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, References |
| `academic-research-conference` | Conference Paper | Abstract, Intro, Background, Approach, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, References |
| `academic-research-discussion` | Discussion Paper | Intro, Context, Argument 1–2, Counterarguments, Conclusion, References |
| `academic-research-methodology` | Methodology Section Builder | Design, Participants, Materials, Procedure, Data Analysis, Ethics |
| `academic-research-results-discussion` | Results/Discussion Structure | Results 1–3, Discussion (Interpretation, Limitations, Implications) |
| `academic-research-reference-heavy` | Reference-Heavy Paper | Intro, Section 1–3 (heavy citation), Conclusion, References |

## Planning Sections (Research Types)

- **Research Question** – Clear, focused, answerable.
- **Thesis / Hypothesis** – Proposed answer or claim.
- **Literature Notes** – Key sources. Gaps in research.
- **Methodology Notes** – Approach, design, data sources.
- **Full Outline** – Section-by-section plan.
- **Sources & References** – Vault/reference manager.
- **Reference List / Bibliography** – Export-ready.
- **Revision Notes** – Clarity, citation accuracy.
