# AUTHORA User Guide

Quick reference for authors using AUTHORA to write and finish their books.

## Getting Started

1. **Create a project** – A project holds one or more books (e.g. a novel, a series, or a non-fiction book).
2. **Add a book** – Choose a template (fiction or non-fiction) or start from scratch.
3. **Build your outline** – Add chapters in the manuscript sidebar. One sentence per chapter is enough to start.
4. **Start writing** – Click a chapter to open the editor. Type or use Ghostwriter to generate a draft.

## Fiction Writing

- **Templates**: Romance, thriller, fantasy, mystery. Each includes setup questions and suggested structure.
- **Outline**: Add chapters and briefs. The AI uses briefs to generate drafts in Ghostwriter mode.
- **Ghostwriter**: Light (suggestions), Heavy (full drafts), or Full (AI handles most drafting). You stay in control.

## Non-Fiction Writing

- **Templates**: Memoir, business, self-help, how-to. Setup questions for audience and key takeaways.
- **Structure**: Parts and chapters. Link research notes to chapters.
- **Reference panel**: Definitions, synonyms, readability. Select text to analyze.

## AI Features

- **Select text** – Rewrite, expand, shorten, change tone, or continue writing.
- **AI panel** – Open from the toolbar. Full menu of actions.
- **API key** – Add your OpenAI or Anthropic key in Settings → AI. Keys are stored securely.

## Accountability

- **Word goals** – Set daily or weekly targets. We count manuscript words only.
- **Style** – Gentle, Balanced, or Structured. Choose how we nudge you.
- **Recovery** – Missed a day? We suggest recovery plans. No guilt.

## Export

- **Formats**: DOCX, PDF, EPUB, TXT.
- **Options**: Title page, table of contents, author name.
- **Publishing prep**: Synopsis, blurb, query letter. From Export → Publishing prep.

## Tips

- Use **Focus mode** (toolbar) for distraction-free writing.
- **Notes** – Link to chapters for easy reference while writing.
- **Version history** – Restore any previous version of a chapter.
