# AUTHORA Pricing Page Update

## 1. Pricing Page Summary

The pricing page has been updated with new feature positioning to clearly present value and drive upgrades. Copy is benefit-focused, uses simple language, and avoids technical jargon.

**Tiers:** Free, Starter, Pro, Studio, Founder Lifetime

**Key changes:**
- **Free** – Emphasizes basic writing, limited AI, starter templates
- **Pro** – Full writing system, story guidance, AI assistance, templates, export
- **Studio** – Citation system, bibliography tools, academic templates, originality review, AI insights, premium features

**Comparison table** – Added 5 new Studio-only rows: citation system, bibliography tools, academic templates, originality review, AI insights and transparency.

---

## 2. Tier Breakdown

### Free
- **Value note:** Get started and explore
- **Description:** Basic writing, light AI, and starter templates. Perfect for trying Authora.
- **Features:**
  - Basic writing and organization
  - One book to get started
  - Limited AI when you need a nudge
  - Starter templates
  - Notes and idea capture
  - Writing goals and progress
  - Basic exports

### Starter
- **Value note:** For building consistency
- **Features:** Up to 3 books, full editor, planning, vault, export, reminders, stronger AI, accountability

### Pro (Recommended)
- **Value note:** Finish your books
- **Description:** Full writing system with story guidance, AI assistance, and everything you need to finish.
- **Features:**
  - Full writing system — write as many books as you need
  - Story guidance — find what your story is missing
  - AI assistance — rewrite, expand, get unstuck
  - Full template library — fiction, nonfiction, poetry, and more
  - Export to DOCX, PDF, EPUB
  - Research vault — ideas and world-building in one place
  - Collaboration — share with beta readers and co-writers
  - Deeper accountability and milestones

### Studio (Premium)
- **Value note:** For academic and serious writers
- **Description:** Everything in Pro, plus citation tools, academic templates, originality review, and AI insights.
- **Features:**
  - Everything in Pro
  - Citation system — add and manage references
  - Bibliography tools — build and format your sources
  - Academic templates — essays, research, course materials
  - Originality review — similarity and AI-assistance transparency
  - AI insights — see where AI helped, review with confidence
  - Premium features — highest AI allowance, priority support

### Founder Lifetime
- **Value note:** Limited early-adopter offer
- **Features:** Lifetime access, Finish Mode, planning workflows, AI support
