# Homepage Copy Summary

## Core Positioning

**AUTHORA** is the writing studio that helps people plan, write, and finish books with structure, momentum, accountability, and AI support in one place.

Primary brand angle: **Finish the book.**

Secondary angle: A guided writing journey for fiction and non-fiction authors.

## Brand Tone

- Premium, warm, inspiring, supportive
- Polished, creative, motivating, trustworthy
- Never cheesy, never overhyped
- Not robotic, not generic

## Target Users

- First-time authors
- Aspiring writers
- Fiction and non-fiction authors
- Memoir and business/authority-book writers
- People with ideas stuck in their head
- Writers who start but struggle to finish
- Users who want AI support without losing their voice

## Key Messages

1. **Finish the book** — The main promise. Most people struggle to finish, not start.
2. **Real editor included** — A manuscript workspace, not just an AI chat box.
3. **AI that helps without taking over** — Your voice stays at the center.
4. **Accountability and Finish Mode** — Core differentiators for completion.
5. **Fiction and non-fiction** — Support for novels, memoirs, business books, workbooks, and more.
6. **Start free** — Primary CTA throughout. No credit card required.

## Trust Strip

Plan clearly. Write smoothly. Finish confidently.
