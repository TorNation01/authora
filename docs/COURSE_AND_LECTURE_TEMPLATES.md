# Course and Lecture Templates

## Overview

10 templates for educators: lecture notes, scripts, lesson plans, course modules, handouts, tutorials, workshops, workbooks, reading responses, and discussion guides. Course-friendly layouts with learning objectives and engagement activities.

## Template List

| Slug | Name | Structure |
|------|------|-----------|
| `academic-course-lecture-notes` | Lecture Notes | Topic, Objectives, Key Concepts, Content Outline, Examples, Summary, References |
| `academic-course-lecture-script` | Lecture Script | Opening, Section 1–3, Transitions, Closing |
| `academic-course-lesson-plan` | Lesson Plan | Title, Objectives, Materials, Starter, Main Activity, Practice, Plenary, Differentiation, Homework |

## Planning Sections (Course Types)

- **Learning Objectives** – Action verbs (analyze, apply, evaluate).
- **Key Concepts** – Core ideas. Prerequisites.
- **Activities & Engagement** – Discussion, exercises, group work.
- **Materials & Resources** – Readings, slides, handouts.
- **Revision Notes** – Clarity, flow, timing.

| Hook | Purpose |

## Course-Friendly Layouts

- **Lecture notes** – Objectives first, content outline, examples, takeaways
- **Lesson plan** – Starter → Main → Practice → Plenary
- **Workshop** – Icebreaker, activities, debrief
- **Workbook** – Unit-based with exercises and self-assessment
