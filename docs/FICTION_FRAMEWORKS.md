# Fiction Frameworks

AUTHORA supports these fiction frameworks at launch.

## Three-Act Structure

**Slug**: `three_act`  
**Ideal for**: Plot-driven novels, commercial fiction, screenplays  
**Genres**: General Fiction, Thriller, Mystery, Science Fiction, Fantasy, Contemporary

### Planning stages
- Setup → Inciting Incident → First Major Turn → Rising Conflict → Midpoint → Escalating Complications → Darkest Moment → Climax → Resolution

### Beat stages (Acts)
- Act I: setup, inciting, first_turn
- Act II: rising, midpoint, complications, darkest
- Act III: climax, resolution

### Outputs
- Act planner, beat cards, chapter roadmap, pacing reminders
- Revision checklist for act balance (roughly 25% / 50% / 25%)

---

## Hero's Journey

**Slug**: `hero_journey`  
**Ideal for**: Epic fantasy, adventure, coming-of-age, mythic tales  
**Genres**: Fantasy, Science Fiction, Young Adult, Adventure

### Planning stages
- Ordinary World → Call to Adventure → Refusal → Mentor → Crossing Threshold → Tests/Allies/Enemies → Approach → Ordeal → Reward → Road Back → Resurrection → Return with the Elixir

### Beat stages (Phases)
- Departure: ordinary_world, call, refusal, mentor, threshold
- Initiation: tests, approach, ordeal, reward
- Return: road_back, resurrection, elixir

### Outputs
- Journey map, transformation tracker
- Character-growth prompts, mythic arc notes

---

## Save-the-Cat Beat Structure

**Slug**: `save_the_cat`  
**Ideal for**: Commercial fiction, screenplays, genre novels  
**Genres**: Thriller, Romance, Mystery, Contemporary, Young Adult

### Planning stages (15 beats)
- Opening Image → Theme Stated → Setup → Catalyst → Debate → Break into Two → B Story → Fun and Games → Midpoint → Bad Guys Close In → All Is Lost → Dark Night of the Soul → Break into Three → Finale → Final Image

### Outputs
- Beat sheet board, pacing guidance
- Revision checklist: opening mirrors final, theme payoff, fun and games delivers

---

## Romance Beat Structure

**Slug**: `romance_beats`  
**Ideal for**: Romance novels  
**Genres**: Romance

### Planning stages
- Meet/Hook → Attraction → Resistance → Emotional Bond → Conflict Escalation → Break/Apart → Vulnerability → Reconciliation → Payoff (HEA/HFN)

### Outputs
- Relationship arc tracker
- Chemistry scene prompts, emotional stakes prompts
- Romance pacing guidance, chapter beat map

---

## Mystery / Thriller Structure

**Slug**: `mystery_thriller`  
**Ideal for**: Crime, suspense, investigation-driven plots  
**Genres**: Thriller, Mystery, Crime, Suspense

### Planning stages
- Crime/Problem Introduced → Stakes Established → Clue Trail → Suspects/Complications → Reveals and Reversals → False Solution → Major Twist → Confrontation → Final Resolution

### Outputs
- Investigation board, clue tracker, suspect tracker
- Reveal planning, tension pacing prompts
- Red-herring checklist

---

## Series Fiction Arc Structure

**Slug**: `series_arc`  
**Ideal for**: Multi-book series, recurring cast, world continuity  
**Genres**: Fantasy, Science Fiction, Romance, Series

### Planning stages
- Book-level arc, series-level arc, recurring cast, world/lore continuity, long-form stakes, cliffhanger/payoff logic

### Outputs
- Per-book framework overlays
- Continuity boards, series arc tracker
- Unresolved thread tracker

---

## Character-Driven Literary Structure

**Slug**: `character_driven`  
**Ideal for**: Literary fiction, character-focused narratives  
**Genres**: Literary Fiction, Contemporary, Women's Fiction

### Planning stages
- Emotional movement, relationships, internal conflict, theme, symbolic development, subtle turning points

### Outputs
- Emotional arc board
- Character tension prompts, reflection and theme prompts
- Slower-burn pacing support

---

## Custom Fiction Structure

**Slug**: `custom_fiction`  
**Ideal for**: Experienced writers, experimental fiction  
**Genres**: Any

Minimal scaffolding. Single planning stage "Your Structure."
