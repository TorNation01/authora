# AUTHORA FAQ

## Product

**What is AUTHORA?**  
A guided writing platform that helps you finish your book. Planning, editor, AI assistance, accountability, and export—all in one place.

**Is there a free tier?**  
Yes. You can start writing for free. No credit card required.

**What formats can I export?**  
DOCX, PDF, EPUB, and plain text. One click from the editor or Export center.

## Writing

**How does the AI work?**  
You control when and how much. Select text for rewrite, expand, shorten, or continue. Ghostwriter mode generates drafts from your outline and briefs. Your voice leads; AI assists.

**Do you train on my content?**  
No. Your text is sent to the AI provider only when you trigger an action. We do not train models on your content.

**Can I use my own AI API key?**  
Yes. Add OpenAI or Anthropic key in Settings → AI. Keys are stored securely.

## Accountability

**How do word goals work?**  
Set daily or weekly targets. We count words from your manuscript (notes and outlines don't count). Choose your nudge style: Gentle, Balanced, or Structured.

**Can I pause reminders?**  
Yes. In Accountability → Settings, enable "Pause plan" when you need a break.

## Technical

**Where is my data stored?**  
On our servers (or your own if self-hosted). See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and your privacy policy.

**Can I self-host AUTHORA?**  
Yes. See [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md) and [ARCHITECTURE-PRODUCTION.md](ARCHITECTURE-PRODUCTION.md).

**What browsers are supported?**  
Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (recent versions). JavaScript required.
